Senior Software Engineering Project

DTPCM: Digital Twin for Predictive Construction Management

A construction management application concept designed to support project monitoring, resource management, and the identification of potential construction delays and risks.

Flutter Dart Supabase Firebase Digital Twin

01 / Overview

About the Project

DTPCM explores how digital technologies can support the management of large construction projects. The application brings project information and operational data together to help users monitor progress and identify potential issues.

The project focuses on construction progress, workers, tasks, materials, equipment, and sensor data, with the goal of supporting better-informed project decisions.

02 / Purpose

Problem & Objectives

The Challenge

Construction teams need clear and timely information about project progress, resources, and potential delays to coordinate work effectively.

Our Objective

Explore a digital solution that centralizes construction information and supports monitoring and risk identification.

Predictive Approach

Investigate how project and sensor data could be used to identify potential resource shortages, equipment issues, and schedule delays.
